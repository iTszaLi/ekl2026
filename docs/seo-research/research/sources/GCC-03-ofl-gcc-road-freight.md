## Why Road Freight is Essential in the Kingdom

With Saudi Arabia at the heart of the GCC, road freight is the most flexible and cost-effective way to move cargo between Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, and across borders into the UAE, Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar and Oman. As a leading freight forwarding company in Riyadh, OFL International offers end-to-end land transport that integrates seamlessly with our air and sea freight services.

- **FTL (Full Truckload)** for dedicated, time-sensitive shipments across Saudi Arabia and the GCC.
- **LTL (Less than Truckload)** consolidations for cost-efficient shared transport.
- **Cross-border trucking** to UAE, Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman and Jordan — fully cleared.
- **Specialized equipment** — flatbeds, low-beds, reefers and curtainsiders for any cargo profile.
