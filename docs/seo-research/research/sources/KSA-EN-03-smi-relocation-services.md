## **1\. International Relocation**

* * *

Door-to-door moving services across continents, tailored for expats, returning citizens, diplomats, and executives.

Includes:

- Free pre-move survey and consultation
- Packing, crating, and handling by trained crews
- Customs clearance and documentation at both origin & destination
- Air, sea, or land freight (FCL/LCL)
- Destination unpacking and setup
- Insurance and value protection options
- Storage solutions (short/long term)

We manage relocations from Europe, the U.S., Asia, GCC, and beyond to Saudi Arabia and vice versa.

## **2\. Domestic Relocation (Within Saudi Arabia)**

* * *

Seamless moves between cities or regions in the Kingdom — Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Eastern Province, and more.

Includes:

- Full or partial packing
- Dismantling and reassembly of furniture
- Secure transportation with GPS tracking
- Floor protection, labeling, and handling for high-value items
- Coordination with building management if needed
- Local insurance coverage

Suitable for families, government staff, and corporate employees.
