# Introduction

**The Common Customs Law of the GCC States was issued By Royal Decree No. M/41 dated** **03/11/1423 H (16/01/2003), which unified customs procedures and laws in the GCC Countries.** **Were unified in accordance with international conventions on governing customs activities.** **The Law regulates several customs procedures, including the procedures for practicing the** **profession of customs clearance, under Article 113 of the Common Customs Law.**

## The purpose of these rules is to establish the procedures and requirements governing the

## profession of customs clearance, and define customs activities and the stages that goods

**undergo upon arrival at customs ports, whether related to the import, export, or transit of goods.** **The present rules also establish the regulations for practicing customs clearance activities by** **classifying the activities and specifying the terms and conditions necessary for practicing each** **activity. Additionally, the rules also aim to control and monitor customs clearance activities for** **incoming, outgoing, and transit goods, in addition to organizing the management of relations** **between the Authority, customs clearance facilities, and customers by specifying the obligations** **of the establishment and the Authority, in order to enhance the efficiency of the customs** **clearance sector.**

# Chapter One: Introductory Provisions

## Article (1): Definitions

## In implementation of the provisions stated herein, the subsequent words and terms shall have

## the meanings ascribed thereto, unless the context otherwise requires:

## The Authority: Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority.

## The Governor: Governor of Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority.

## The Common Customs Law: The Common Customs Law of the GCC States approved by Royal

## Decree No. M/41 dated 03/11/1423 H (16/01/2003).

## The Rules: The Rules of Practicing Customs Clearance Profession.

## Customs Clearance: Documenting customs data for incoming, outgoing, and in-transit goods in

## accordance with customs procedures stipulated in the Common Customs Law.

## License: Approved document issued by the Authority authorizing the entity practicing customs

## clearance activities after being activated.

## Legal Entity: Each business entity licensed by the Authority to practice customs clearance.

## Classification Broker: Employee within the entity specializing in classifying goods in accordance

**with customs tariffs. This employee is the only one authorized to create customs data in the** **Authority systems.** **Clearance Broker: Employee within the entity specializing in monitoring the customs data** **procedures requiring Authority review, such as sampling or inspection procedures.** **Special Representative: Employee of a public/ private sector entity specializing in classifying** **goods in accordance with customs tariffs and completing clearance procedures on behalf of the** **entity.** **Bank Guarantee: A document issued by a bank licensed by the Saudi Central Bank to guarantee** **the payment of a specified sum, with the guarantee being unconditional.**

---

**Insurance Policy: A policy issued by an insurer licensed by the Saudi Central Bank to cover the** **customs activities amount, in accordance with requirements Established by the Authority.**

## Documentary Credit: A letter of credit that meets the criteria Established by the Authority.

## Import and Export Activity: The activity authorizing the entity to carry out customs procedures

**on behalf of clients for goods entering and leaving the KSA in accordance with the Common** **Customs Law.**

## Transit Activity: The activity authorizing the entity to transport goods through the KSA in

## accordance with the provisions of the Common Customs Law and applicable international

**conventions.**

## Express Transport Activity: The process of transporting parcels and postal consignments within

## the KSA or between the KSA and other countries in accordance with the requirements and

## controls issued by the competent authorities in the KSA.

## Clients: Importers, exporters and the like.

**Customs Zone: The area designated by the Minister of Finance in each sea, air, or land port or** **any other administrative centre where all or some of customs procedures are authorized to be** **completed.**

## The Academy: The department Specialized in training programs and certification within the

**Authority.**

## Toxicology Screen Certificate: A certificate Issued by health entities approved by the Ministry

**Of Health In the Kingdom Of Saudi Arabia stating that the applicant is not under the influence of** **toxic or narcotic substances.**

## Criminal Record Certificate : A certificate issued by the competent security authorities in the

## KSA, attesting that the applicant has not previously been condemned by Final judgment for

**reasons associated with the abuse of power, or convicted by a conclusive judgment for a crime** **related to customs smuggling or any other crime, unless the applicant has been rehabilitated or** **five years have elapsed since the sentence was issued.**

## Article (2): Application Scope

## Without prejudice to applicable laws and regulations, all entities and public and private sector

## Entities and agencies involved in customs clearance shall be bound by these Rules.

# Chapter Two: Issuance and Renewal

## Article (3): License Issuance and Renewal

**The license shall be valid for (3) years and renewed for a similar period. The entity desiring to** **practice any of the customs clearance activities shall apply to the Authority for the issuance or** **renewal of a license in accordance with the conditions set forth in this Article.**

## I: Customs clearance license requirements:

## 1. Apply for issuing customs clearance license through channels approved by the Authority;

## 2. present a valid commercial register that includes “customs clearance activities.” Commercial

## entities holding a valid unified logistics license issued by the competent authority are

## exempted from this requirement;

## 3. Adding any of the activities mentioned in Article 9 hereof.

## Second: Customs clearance license renewal requirements:

## 4. Apply for the renewal of the customs clearance license through channels approved by the

**Authority;**

**5. submit a valid commercial register containing “customs clearance activities.” Clients holding** **a valid unified logistics license issued by the competent authority are exempted from this** **requirement.**

# Chapter Three: Licenses

## Article (4): Customs Clearance Licenses Categories

## A. General Clearance License: Allows the entity to engage in customs clearance for one or

## more of the following activities:

## 1. Import and export activity.

## 2. Transit activity.

## 3. Transportation of postal packages.

## B. Special Clearance License: permits public and private sectors authorities to perform customs

## clearance activities only for their inbound and outbound shipments, subject to the existence

## of an authorization for an employee working for such authority in accordance with the

## requirements described herein.

## Article (5): Conditions for Customs Clearance Employees Working In Customs

## Clearance Entities

## Entity desiring to engage in any customs clearance activities shall provide at least one broker

**or more for customs clearance and at lat least one broker or more for customs classification.** **Employees in customs clearance entities may be simultaneously designated to these two** **professions after meeting the requirements outlined below for each occupation listed belowI:** **Clearance Broker:** **The clearance broker shall:**

**1. Be either a Saudi either or a national of a GCC state, residing in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.**
## 2. Be at least (18) years old.

---

## 3. Have, at least, a high school diploma or its equivalent.

**4. Be an entity employee as per the records of the General Organization for Social Insurance** **(GOSI).**
## 5. Not own another active licensed entity, and may only practice the profession within that

**entity.**

**6. Provide a toxicology screen certificate.**
## 7. Provide a criminal record certificate.

## II: Classification Broker:

## The person undertaking such task shall:

**1. Be either a Saudi or a national of a GCC state,who is a resident of in the Kingdom of Saudi** **Arabia.**
## 2. Be at least (18) years old.

## 3. Have, at least, a high school diploma or its equivalent.

**4. Be an entity owner or employee as per the records of the General Organization for Social** **Insurance.**
## 5. Not own another active licensed entity, and may only practice the profession within that

**entity.**

**6. Provide a toxicology screen certificate.**
## 7. Provide a criminal record certificate.

## 8. Pass professional examinations and evaluation requirements administered by the Academy

## or any other Authority-approved agency. Those who have worked in the clearance and

## customs procedures field for at least five years may be exempted from such examinations.

---

## Article (6): Special Representative

## Public and private sector agencies have the right to carry out imports and/or exports customs

## procedures by authorizing an employee per the following requirements:

## 1. Representative must be at least (18) years old.

## 2. Representative must not own an entity with an active clearance license, and may only

## practice the profession of a special Representative for the agency authorizing him or her to

## carry out procedures on their behalf.

## 3. Representative must be an employee of the agency per the records of the General

## Organization for Social Insurance; diplomatic agencies are excluded.

## 4. An authorization from the owner of the goods detailing the special Representative's

## information shall be provided to the Authority.

## 5. Provide a toxicology screen certificate must be submitted; diplomatic and government

## agencies’ employees are exempted .

## 6. Provide a criminal record certificate must be submitted; diplomatic agencies employees are

**excluded.**

**7. A final discharge certificate must be submitted (in case of being previously employed at** **another entity).**
## 8. Pass professional examinations and evaluation requirements administrated by the Academy

## or any other Authority-approved agency. Those who have worked in the clearance and

## customs procedures field for at least five years may be exempted from such examinations.

## Article (7): Continual Professional Qualification and Assessment

## The Authority may perform periodic assessments on the classification brokers and the special

## Representative, or conduct qualification courses and workshops to confirm their proficiency and

## expertise in all aspects of their duties and responsibilities. Such activities aim to enhance their

## professional and academic aptitude according to the guidelines of the Academy.

# Chapter Four: Customs Clearance Activities

## Article (8): License Activation

## in case of a license activation, entity shall:

## 1. Add one or more activities from the following:

## a. Export and import activity.

## b. Transit activity.

## c. Express transportation activity.

## 2. specify the port(s) where the selected activity will be performed.

## Article (9): Requirements of Customs Clearance Activities

**I: Entity may conduct one or more activities in a port and may designate one or more classification** **brokers at several ports.** **II. Entity shall comply with the following requirements for the inclusion or renewal of customs** **clearance activities:**

**1. Export and import activity:**
## a. Select the port at which the activity is intended to be exercised.

## b. Provide a documentary guarantee.

## c. Appoint a classification broker in accordance with the requirements outlined in Article 5 of

## these Rules.

## 2. Transit activity:

## a. Select the port at where the activity is intended to be conducted.

## b. Agree to cover, under all circumstances, any deficiency in the required transit guarantee.

## c. Appoint a classification broker in accordance with the requirements outlined in Article 5 of

## these Rules.

---

**d. Provide a bank guarantee or insurance policy in the Authority's name for a minimum of** **fifty thousand Saudi riyal, provided that the bank guarantee/insurance policy remains in** **effect for at least one year beyond the license term.**
## 3. Express transport activity:

## a. Acquire a postal parcel international transport license issued from the competent authority.

**b. Select the port at which the activity is intended to be conducted , provided that such port** **shall be one of the customs ports which allow the conduct of express transport activities.**
## c. Provide a documentary guarantee of fulfilling any financial obligation concerning the

**activity.**

**d. Appoint a classification broker in accordance with the requirements outlined in Article 5 of** **these Rules.**
## III. Documentary guarantees may be granted for transit activity if any of the following conditions

## are met:

## 1. If the entity acquired an economic operator license approved by the Authority.

**2. If the entity is being licensed to exercise express transport activity by the competent authority.**
## 3. If the entity is a joint stock company listed in the Saudi Capital Market.

## 4. If the entity obtained a unified logistics license from the competent authority. This

## requirement shall be limited to activities of export and transport.

## IV. The activity may be renewed for a duration not exceeding the license's expiration date.

# Chapter Five: Obligations

## Article (10): Entity Obligations

## Entity and employees shall:

## 1. Comply with all laws and instructions issued by the Authority or other relevant authorities

## pertaining to the activities being carried out.

## 2. Maintain the confidentiality of information, data and passwords used to access the Authority’s

## e-system and any other associated e-system. Entity and/or employees shall prohibit access

## to and/or use of the same at all times.

## 3. Maintain the confidentiality of their clients’ commercial transactions, and shall exercise due

## diligence in the performance of their duties.

## 4. Provide automatic customs declaration immediately and effectively following the receipt

## of documents required for clearance. Moreover, entity and/or its employees shall archive

## documents online, translate invoices into Arabic or English, if needed, elucidate the description

## of each item individually, and list such items under the applicable clause.

## 5. Provide an e-system through which all customs activities, as well as other client-related

## activities, are registered, provided that such data be retained for five years at least.

## 6. Perform the assigned customs-clearance transactions immediately following automatic

**registration. If there is a reason for the delay in carrying out any procedures, the entity shall** **notify the customs zone and the affected person in writing or via email. The entity may not** **discontinue the follow-up of carrying out the procedures without providing a justification** **acceptable to the Authority.**

**7. Provide the owner of the goods with a copy of the invoice for expenses and costs incurred** **as a result of the clearance of the goods, as well as a copy of the customs declaration and** **customs payment fixed receipts, as well as any other fees or expenses paid for this purpose.**
## 8. Deliver any shipment whose procedures have been completed to the agreed-upon delivery

**point off the client and entity have agreed to provide other transport services in addition to** **clearance. Entity may not, in any way, withhold, conceal, or confiscate the shipment.**

---

## 9. Adhere to standards of professionalism and honesty in its dealings with clients. The Authority

## may issue any rules or instructions pertaining to these employees.

## 10. Cooperate with the employees of the Authority during inspection visits and provide them

## with all necessary documents.

## 11. Conduct customs clearance operations using authorized personnel. Entity may not assign or

## authorize external personnel for this activity.

## 12. Commitment to contracting with all employees of the entity in accordance with the Labor

## Law and other applicable laws. Entity shall also ensure a sufficient number of employees

## for the performance of required tasks, and the Authority may, as appropriate, establish

## classification standards and criteria to ensure operational efficiency and improve quality of

## the provided services.

## 13. Comply with Labor Law and applicable regulations and decisions when terminating the

## services of an employees. Entity shall also report to the Authority via the authorized

## communication channel immediately after terminating the employment contracts of such

**employees.**

## 14. Customs clearance entity shall fulfil its obligations and restrictions. within ten (10) business

**days.**

**15. All employees of customs clearance entities shall report any suspicion of infraction of the** **public order to the public order applicable in the KSA or any customs operation by their clients** **or customers.**
## 16. Custom clearance entity shall complete all license renewal procedures and activities with

## fifteen (15) days before the date of expiration.

**17. Comply with the detailed description when collecting and entering data in all sections of the** **customs declaration based on the documents provided by the client.**
**,**

---

**18. Custom clearance entity shall be liable for any obligations owed to the Authority if a delay is** **caused by it or any of its employees. This shall apply unless it is proven that the delay was** **caused by circumstances beyond the control of the entity or its customer, or if the Authority** **accepts the justification in each case, procedure or circumstance. delay's**
## 19. Comply with the customs requirements regarding the request of presence of a zone's customs

## clearance broker by the specified date.

## Article (11): Employees Obligations

## Entity’s employees shall:

## 1. Comply with the provisions of the Common Customs Law, the instructions issued by the

## Authority, and these Rules.

## 2. Cooperate with employees of the customs zone and observe standard work ethics.

**3. Refrain from working for and/or at any entity other than the entity for which they are permitted** **to work.**
## 4. Maintain the privacy and confidentiality of all client and customs clearance entity data,

## information and passwords. Moreover, entity employees shall use the Authority›s or

## relevant agencies› systems accurately and appropriately.

## 5. Act transparently, in accordance with disclosure policies, and in accordance with the

## applicable regulations.

---

## Article (12): Authority Obligations towards the Entity

## 1. The classification broker and special representative shall be granted access to clearance

## activity e-system and all other relevant necessary powers to administer the account of the

## entity and/or its affiliated agencies.

## 2. Entity employees are entitled to receive and automatically activate a customs portal access

**card.**

## 3. All systems, special models, and training materials related to the practice of customs

## clearance shall be made available, and any amendments thereon shall be published through

## official channels of communication.

## 4. The Authority shall conduct routine inspections of customs clearance entities to ensure their

## compliance with customs clearance regulatory instructions and to evaluate the performance

## of customs clearance personnel.

## 5. Requests and complaints of clearance entities shall be received, and suggestions and

## proposals submitted via Authorized communication channels shall be reviewed, along with

## the progress in accordance with the Authority's adopted service level.

# Chapter Six: General Provisions

## Article (13): Terms of Customs Clearance Offices

**1. A customs clearance entity shall provide at least one office in accordance with the type of** **activity intended to be conducted, provided that such office is independent and licensed by** **the competent authority under a lease agreement or deed of ownership to conduct customs** **clearance.**
## 2. Appropriate premises, requirements, and necessities must be available to receive and serve

## clients. In addition, devices permitting access to the Authority's e-system around the clock

## must be provided.

## 3. An instructive and informative panel shall be placed.

## Article (14): License General Provisions

## 1. Entity shall acquire an e-authorization from clients before conducting customs clearance on

## their behalf.

**2. Customs clearance shall only be performed on behalf of a client after obtaining the required** **license from the Authority in accordance with the terms and conditions outlined in these** **Rules.**
**,**

**3. The Authority has the right to revoke any entity s license if it has been inactive for (180)** **consecutive days, and any fees owed to the Authority must be paid.**
## 4. Customs clearance licenses may not be rented nor waived -without the Authority’s approval-

## and if either of these actions were taken, the license shall be revoked.

## 5. If an entity does not communicate with its employees regarding goods-clearance finalization

## procedures for unjustified reasons, the Authority shall have the right to transfer finalization

## authorization to another entity at the request of the affected person without any liability

**whatsoever.**

---

## 6. The Authority may determine the number of licenses and activities based on actual business

**requirements.**

**7. Renewal of license does not include renewal of activities, and the customs clearance entity** **must renew the activity intended to be exercised in accordance with the provisions of these** **Rules.**
## 8. Additional bank guarantee shall be returned to cover any discrepancies that may arise after

## the complete and lawful exit of the goods from the port.

**9. The Authority may return the bank guarantee to the complied entities in accordance with the** **requirements it establishes.**
# Chapter Seven: Termination and Cancellation

## Article (15): License and/or Related Activities Cancellation Procedures

## Any customs clearance entity who desires to cancel a customs clearance license or any thereof

## activities shall submit a cancellation request per the following:

## 1. Submission of a cancellation request clarifying the activity intended to be cancelled.

## 2. Settlement of any financial or documentary obligations entitled to the customs clearance

## entity or any thereof employees. After that, port access cards shall be returned and user

## code and password be deactivated.

## Article (16): Employees Transfer Procedures

**,** **The following requirements shall be met for an employee s transfer from one entity to another:**

**1. Submitting a transfer request to the Authority via the Authorized channels.**
## 2. Returning the access cards of the original customs yards to the Authority and deactivating

## the user ID on the e-system upon transfer or termination of business.

## 3. Submitting the final discharge certificate provided from the employing entity.

---

## Article (17): License Cancellation Cases

## A license shall be cancelled in any of the following cases:

## 1. Company Termination and Liquidation or stricken a sole proprietorship from the Commercial

## Register for any reason whatsoever.

**2. Violation of the customs clearance regulations and laws after the issuance of a final decision** **of conviction from the customs committees or competent courts, after issuance of a final** **decision of conviction with regard to customs smuggling, or after issuance of a final judgment** **of conviction -from the competent court- enforcing a custodial penalty due to the indulgence** **in a moral tribute crime.**
## 3. Remaining inactive for a period of (180) consecutive days.

## 4. Issuance of a judicial ruling enforcing the liquidation, bankruptcy, or enjoinment of the entity;

## or if the entity is unable to pursue customs clearance activities in any way.

## 5. Issuance of a decision from the competent authority enforcing the cancelation of activity or

## commercial register.

**6. If the license is proved to be rented to, used by, or assigned to a third party by the entity** **without the Authority’s approval.**
## 7. If the activity remains undefined after (180) days from license issuance date.

## 8. If the customs clearance entity requests cancellation of the customs clearance license.

# Chapter Eight: Final Provisions

## Article (18): Penalties

**1. According to Articles (154) and (157) of the Common Customs Law, the entity and its** **employees are jointly and severally liable for any customs violations and smuggling crimes** **committed by its authorized and/or affiliated users.**
## 2. Disciplinary sanctions specified in Article (114) of the Common Customs Law shall be applied

**if any provision of these Rules, the Common Customs Law, or any related law is violated by** **the entity or its employees. After conducting a thorough investigation into the culpable party,** **such penalties shall be applied.**

**3. Subject to Paragraph (2) of this Article, the Authority may apply penalties and fines for** **customs infractions committed by the entity or its employees in accordance with the** **provisions of Article (14) of the Common Customs Law.**
## Article (19): Objections

**,** **The entity is notified of the penalties and/or sanction through the Authority s official channels** **of communication. Entity may contest such penalties and/or sanction in accordance with the** **regulatory procedures outlined in Articles (114) and (148) of the Common Customs Law.**

## Article (20): Entry to force

**The present Rules shall be promulgated and amended by a decision of the Governor, and become** **effective one hundred twenty (120) days after publication in the official gazette. Such Rules shall** **supersede the conflicting provisions and decisions.**
