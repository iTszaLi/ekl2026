# Exhibitions and Events

We provide extraordinary services for events and exhibitions. We have worked with top event management companies in the region to move their unique artefacts across the Middle East. We have also worked with major Saudi corporates on their exhibitions in Saudi Arabia and their participation in international exhibitions — particularly as Saudi Arabia is not part of the ATA Carnet system. We combine our experienced customs compliance team with our talented crew in fixing and packing exhibition items to be the preferred Saudi logistics company for exhibitions.

## What we can offer you

- Temporary export for exhibition items, including mock-ups and models
- Preparing all necessary documents and filing them with customs
- The design, construction and deconstruction of exhibitions, stage decoration and lighting fixtures
- Professional packing and logistics of high-value items such as fine art
- Temporary import for parties who do not have a legal commercial presence in Saudi Arabia but wish to exhibit their products or services
- Door-to-door international air and sea freight
- On-site attendance and project management
- Aircraft chartering, including VIP charters
- Design and construction of packaging, cases and crates
- Meet-and-greet arrangements handled by our relocation team
