# Competitor gap analysis and prioritization

Checked 17 October 2026. Search results were retrieved separately for English and Arabic. These observations are not a Saudi Google rank tracker, market-share calculation or verification of competitors' operational claims.

## Four distinct evidence classes

1. **Owner-supplied competitor estimates:** all 40 SAL rows, preserved in `sal-competitor-keywords.csv`. Report tool, report date, country filters and device are unspecified. Relative “updated” strings and raw `5.8K` volume are retained. These estimates are time-sensitive; they are not ENVOD's ranks.
2. **Observed live web content:** dated search results and fetched pages below. These establish what those pages describe, not their traffic.
3. **ENVOD first-party data:** unavailable. Do not populate ENVOD rank, traffic, leads or conversion-rate cells with competitor figures.
4. **Research hypotheses:** exact query variants in the opportunity map without measured demand. Unavailable numerical cells stay unavailable.

`sal-keyword-disposition.csv` accounts for all 40 rows. SAL names, careers and investor terms are retained only for benchmarking. Ambiguous “saudi logistics”/“saudi cargo” terms are held for intent checks. Top-ten/list terms are educational research only; they do not justify describing ENVOD as top ten. “logestic” is a low-priority spelling variant, not a page title.

## Preferred URL policy

- Home owns broad provider and ENVOD brand discovery.
- `/services` owns service-catalog intent.
- Existing service pages own specific freight, customs, warehouse and specialist intent. EN and AR variants are equivalents, not competing duplicates.
- City phrases initially map to the existing appropriate service or Home. Add a city page only when actual coverage and unique gateway/process information justify it. Do not invent Jeddah/Dammam offices or warehouse addresses.
- Treat 3PL and last-mile terms as current warehousing content opportunities, pending confirmation of actual order-handling scope. A separate page is conditional, not already launched.
- Domestic transport is currently mapped to the existing transport page. A dedicated page requires verified vehicle options, operator arrangements, routes and useful distinct content.
- Industry pages support their sector's needs and link to service pages; they should not duplicate the service page's primary intent.

## Commercial order

P1: customs/gateway enquiries, freight forwarding, air/ocean freight, exhibition/ATA and genuine brand discovery. P2: verified warehousing/3PL, domestic/GCC transport depth, temperature control and projects. P3: broad dictionary terms, misspellings and specialist topics with unverified demand.

Difficulty and volume alone do not set priority. A low supplied difficulty does not prove an easy ranking opportunity. No overlapping estimated traffic totals are presented.
