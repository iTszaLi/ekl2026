# Initial Saudi search research

Research date: 17 October 2026.

## Method and limits

Used live web search with separate English and Arabic query groups. Results below are pages returned by the research search service, **not verified Google Saudi ranking positions**. No search volume, keyword difficulty, traffic or backlink estimates were obtained. A parallel page-fetch attempt failed with HTTP 500; observations below therefore rely on returned excerpts and require full-page verification before detailed competitor scoring or regulatory copy is published.

## English customs sample

Queries: `Saudi customs clearance company`, `customs clearance Riyadh company`, `Jeddah port customs broker`.

| Observed page | Search excerpt topic | Useful research direction |
|---|---|---|
| https://custom-clearance.saz.sa/en/contact-us | Dammam/Jeddah broker contact, hours, telephone | Make business contact information clear and consistent |
| https://mslc.com.sa/customs-clearance | Saudi brokerage, documentation, Riyadh/Jeddah/Dammam | Explain the enquiry-to-release process without adopting competitor licensing claims |
| https://oflsa.com/customs-clearance | RUH, Riyadh Dry Port, Jeddah and Dammam, classification, conformity | Separate gateway-specific customer questions and document requirements |
| https://scls.co/services/customs-clearance | Customs and trade compliance, government platforms | Distinguish assistance with a platform from official certification |

## Independent Arabic sample

Queries: `شركة تخليص جمركي السعودية`, `تخليص جمركي الرياض`, `تخليص جمركي ميناء جدة`.

| Observed page | Search excerpt topic | Useful research direction |
|---|---|---|
| https://www.neef.sa/ | التخليص والنقل والتخزين وإجراءات المنتجات المنظمة | Link clearance to transport/storage and explain applicable approvals |
| https://rh-logistics.sa/ | Riyadh/Jeddah customs terms and local enquiries | Use natural local terminology, not repeated city-keyword lists |
| https://kdl.com.sa/ | Freight, transport and city-specific customs articles | Examine whether each city article addresses a distinct customer need |
| https://kdl.com.sa/blog/شركة-تخليص-جمركي | Comparison and provider-selection intent | Answer how businesses should prepare and evaluate a provider without claiming to be “best” |

These are observed competitors/content examples, not independently verified endorsements or evidence of their operational claims.

## Official-source leads

- ZATCA import instructions: https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/customs-bussiness/import-pages/Import-Instructions.aspx
  - Returned excerpt mentions commercial invoice, bill of lading, conditional certificate of origin and additional product-specific requirements. Consult the complete current instructions before prescribing a definitive checklist.
- ZATCA seaport procedures: https://eservices.zatca.gov.sa/sites/sc/en/CustomsGuideNew/CustomsGuideseaPorts/Pages/Page01.aspx
  - Returned excerpt describes inspection, document review, collection and release stages; not evidence that a particular broker can guarantee release time.
- ZATCA broker services: https://zatca.gov.sa/en/eServices/Pages/eServices-253.aspx
  - Licensing administration; does not verify ENVOD's licensing.
- ZATCA customs-clearance rules: https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/customs-bussiness/customs-rules.aspx
