# Practical backlink outreach plan

No messages have been sent and no listings acquired. Workflow status starts at **Not contacted — owner approval pending**.

## Weeks 1–2: identity and qualification

- Approve one EN/AR business-information sheet: legal name, genuine contact location, phone, hours, offered services and rights-cleared imagery.
- Check existing profiles and current memberships; never pay twice or replace an owner's account.
- Review the two P1 public submission entry points, duplicates, data fields and fees. Confirm actual website-link availability rather than assuming all citations include a backlink.
- Obtain owner approval for each submission and any spending. Keep legal documents and personal credentials out of public research files.

## Weeks 3–4: small, reviewed submissions

- Prepare one tailored business profile or relevant editorial enquiry at a time, using the publisher's stated route.
- Use the public guide only if it genuinely answers the publisher's audience's needs. Do not demand exact-match anchor text or promise an exchange.
- Record the actual submission date, approval and response. One polite follow-up may be considered after the publisher's stated review period; stop on refusal or opt-out.
- No automation, bulk mail, fake reviewers or outsourced “guaranteed links.”

## Weeks 5–8: authentic industry relationships

- Confirm actual FIATA/JCtrans eligibility or existing membership before profile work.
- Evaluate a future expo only for real business value and an approved participation budget. Ask about contractual listing/advertising terms; do not invent an official appointment.
- Ask existing customers or partners about a factual case study only after the owner identifies and authorizes a real relationship.
- Investigate chamber candidates without creating speculative Riyadh/Jeddah/Dammam premises.

## Weeks 9–12: inspect value, not counts

- For every genuinely published result, inspect final URL, correct details, target link, sponsored/nofollow treatment, accessibility and relevance.
- Record authorized referral traffic and privately qualified enquiries if available. A published citation is not a proven lead or ranking increase.
- Refresh unsuitable/changed opportunities and close rejected or irrelevant items rather than chasing an arbitrary backlink target.

## Approved tracker states

Not contacted → owner-approved → submitted → awaiting review → accepted/published or declined/closed. Use dates only for actions that occurred. Track publication separately from outreach and referral results.

For paid sponsorship/advertorial placement, require suitable advertising disclosure and search-compliant link qualification. No DR/DA guarantees, paid followed-link requirements or bulk-backlink purchases.
