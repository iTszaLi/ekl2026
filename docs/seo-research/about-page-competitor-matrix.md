## Saudi and Arabic-language context

- Official [ZATCA import guidance](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/customs-bussiness/import-pages/Import-Instructions.aspx) states that requirements depend on the goods and required documents; its [November 2024 announcement](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-1311.aspx) refers to submitting import declarations and documents 72 hours before arrival. The About page therefore avoids promising 24-hour clearance or a universal processing time.
- The official [FASAH single-window site](https://www.fasah.sa/trade/sau/html/en_US/subpage-about.html) describes electronic data exchange and trade services among parties involved in import and export. ENVOD copy should not imply that ENVOD is the customs authority or controls platform/authority decisions.
- [SABER's terms](https://saber.sa/Home/Terms?culture=en-US) describe product conformity and consignment certificates for applicable consumer goods. Product applicability is shipment-specific; the page must not present SABER as universally required.
- Arabic official company pages use natural phrases such as **الخدمات اللوجستية**, **الشحن**, **التخليص الجمركي**, and **سلاسل الإمداد**. Use these in meaningful copy where accurate; no search-volume or ranking claim is inferred from the wording.

## ENVOD evidence gate

The workspace and public-site review did not establish primary documentation for the legacy claims “25+ years,” “500+ events,” “24hr ATA processing/clearance,” “50+ countries,” or “98% on-time.” The company-specific membership records for WCAworld, JCtrans, WCA Events, PCN, and FIATA were also not verified. These findings mean “evidence not established in this review,” not that a claim is false or the company is not a member.

The current map is owner-supplied content, not independent evidence of premises, staffing, partner status, fixed routes, or service coverage. Riyadh is identified in the existing workspace as the head office; the other displayed city nodes must not be described as staffed physical branches unless their status is confirmed.
