## Editorial acquisition angles

- Offer an accurate checklist as a supporting reference to a relevant editor, not a paid followed-link exchange.
- For an event organizer, first confirm a real relationship and permission to mention the event. A public event listing is not proof ENVOD served it.
- Develop an anonymized “documents most often missing” study **only after** authorized operational data exists. No invented sample size, turnaround average or success rate.
- Consider a genuine case study with written customer consent, original photos and limitations; omit shipment identifiers and sensitive routes.

## Publication gate

Professional Arabic and a responsible logistics reviewer should review regulated details. Check visible copy against structured data and remove superseded instructions. Refresh source dates, ensure reciprocal EN/AR links, and inspect direct hash links and mobile tables. Any off-site placement still requires owner approval and publisher acceptance.
