## Quality and compliance screen

Before approval, check the actual listing's relevance, indexability, outbound-link treatment, review process, duplicate records, contact-data exposure, terms and commercial value. Inspect a real published example; directory marketing language is not an independent trust certificate.

For paid advertising, advertorials or sponsorship links, ask the publisher for appropriate `rel="sponsored"` (or applicable `nofollow`) treatment. Do not require followed paid links, buy bulk links, use PBNs or submit to irrelevant sites. Membership is a professional/business decision, not a purchased ranking outcome.

Do not scrape employee email lists, send mass mail, manufacture reviews, publish confidential cargo/customer data, or claim a customer/event relationship without evidence.

## Outreach workflow

1. Owner approves legal name, bilingual description, actual address/hours, services and permitted imagery.
2. Search for a duplicate; determine who owns the account and whether the company is eligible.
3. Confirm editorial requirements, fees, publication terms and link attributes.
4. Prepare one tailored submission or editorial enquiry. Obtain approval before sending.
5. Log the actual date, response and URL. Keep “not contacted” until an action really occurs.
6. If published, inspect the final page, link destination/attributes and NAP accuracy. Record referral/lead value only when supported by analytics and private lead qualification.
