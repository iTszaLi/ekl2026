## Full route and phrase coverage — 18 October 2026

The working phrase-level source of truth is `ksa-organic-keyword-database.csv`. The route-level source of truth is `keyword-to-page-map.csv`; it now includes an Arabic URL and primary phrase, EN/AR supporting phrases, content outline, evidence status and editorial priority for each route. The route file contains **46 existing EN routes and their 46 `/ar` equivalents**. No URL was added or changed, and all 18 placeholder primary targets were replaced with distinct page intents.

Across the eight pre-existing phrase CSVs, the audit reviewed **608 source rows**, representing **197 normalized keyword/language pairs** and **411 repeated row appearances across files**. These are overlaps between source files, not 411 bad keywords. The historical source files remain intact. The canonical database grew from 149 records (86 EN, 63 AR) to **566 records (351 EN, 215 AR)**, with **zero exact normalized keyword/language duplicates**. This includes 44 proposed industry phrases (two EN and two AR for each of 11 existing industry routes).

The canonical database marks **65 selected Google Suggest responses** (41 EN, 24 AR) as observed suggestions, not as volume, rankings, or proof of the searcher's location. All other additions are explicitly proposals or legacy candidates. Phrase-level editorial priorities are **25 P1, 523 P2 and 18 P3**; P1 is restricted to a small set of core service and brand terms, not assigned automatically to every variation. Twenty-seven metric-bearing legacy rows retain user-provided SAL competitor estimates with unspecified tool, market and date; these are not ENVOD performance data. Search volume, difficulty and ENVOD rank remain unavailable.

### Disposition and validation

The three explicit low-fit/malformed candidates `how much customs broker make`, `what is a US customs broker`, and `sea lead shipping Saudi Arabia` were not added as ENVOD service targets. Career, competitor-branded and non-Saudi route suggestions were excluded from target recommendations. City office/airport phrases are retained only as low-priority conditional research terms with explicit premises/licence gates. No city doorway URLs were created.

The attached brief's proposed phrases are labeled as proposals; the 65 autocomplete observations are individually labeled with language, seed and observation date. Fresh public SERP/competitor research could not be added on 18 October because the search service returned HTTP 402. The October 17 public observations remain available in `controlled-saudi-search-landscape.md`; none is presented as an ENVOD rank.
