# ENVOD KINGDOM About-page enhancement report

Research date: 23 October 2026

## Scope

Enhance the existing English and Arabic About page in the workspace preview. This is an isolated review of the existing artifact; it does not authorize changes to the public Vercel site or a deployment. The competitor benchmark is documented in [about-page-competitor-matrix.md](about-page-competitor-matrix.md).

## Claims and supporting evidence

The About-page review did not find primary company records that substantiate the site's legacy claims of “25+ years,” “500+ events,” “24hr ATA/customs,” “50+ countries,” or “98% on-time.” They are not used as established facts in the enhanced About content. This is an evidence gap, not a finding that the claims are false.

The same evidence standard applies to “leading” or similar superlatives, company history dates, customer/project success stories, owned assets, service guarantees, licenses, awards, and performance metrics. These require current primary records and owner approval before they can be presented as verified.

## Locations and network

The existing map lists Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Dubai, Manama, and Udupi. Riyadh is identified in the workspace as ENVOD's head office. The map list alone does not independently establish staffed premises, partner status, fixed freight routes, or service availability in each city. The workspace's local visibility audit specifically notes that Jeddah and Dammam premises were not established through public research.

Preserve the listed locations and map interactions, but distinguish the Riyadh head office from other company-listed location references and broader service/network context. Do not infer a branch, partner, route, or guaranteed coverage from a map point or connector.

The About page labels these as management-confirmed wherever the public record or primary supporting document does not independently establish the exact company detail.

No primary TGA licence document, number, holder, activity wording, or current validity was found in the workspace.

A review-time search of FIATA's [Saudi member directory](https://fiata.org/directory/sa/) did not locate ENVOD, and no company-specific membership record or category document was found in the workspace. This is an evidence gap, not a finding that the affiliation is false.

## Saudi operating context

The official [ZATCA import instructions](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/customs-bussiness/import-pages/Import-Instructions.aspx) make requirements dependent on the goods and supporting documents. ZATCA's [November 2024 notice](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-1311.aspx) discusses submitting import declarations and documents 72 hours before arrival. The [FASAH site](https://www.fasah.sa/trade/sau/html/en_US/subpage-about.html) describes electronic data exchange among import/export stakeholders, while [SABER terms](https://saber.sa/Home/Terms?culture=en-US) address conformity and consignment certificates for applicable products. Copy must remain shipment-specific and must not imply that ENVOD controls customs decisions, product approvals, or a universal processing time.

## Implementation and verification

The existing About page was enhanced in English and Arabic in the workspace preview only. The changes add:

- A clear company and shipment-coordination introduction, an undated thematic journey rather than invented founding dates, and shipment-specific descriptions of Saudi-market documentation and customs boundaries.
- Draft purpose, vision, mission, and values, explicitly labeled for owner review rather than presented as approved corporate policy.
- Organized specialist capabilities, operational process guidance, customer FAQs, and a quote-preparation checklist, while retaining existing service, industry, contact, quote, and tracking pathways.
- A prominent bilingual section for JCtrans, TGA freight brokerage, and FIATA, with the exact public profile/info links, management-confirmation wording, and limits on independent verification.
- One already-published, owner-supplied relocation photo in the hero, using responsive gallery WebP sizes and localized descriptive alt text.
- Clearer map wording: Riyadh is identified as the head office; Jeddah, Dammam, Dubai, Manama, and Udupi remain company-listed location references, not asserted branches. The map explains that its connectors are visual guides rather than verified freight routes. The About-only map mode leaves the shared footer map's default behavior unchanged.

The repeated working-principles block was consolidated into Values; all six ideas remain, without the duplicate section. No existing service, industry, location, or page route was removed.
