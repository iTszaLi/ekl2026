# ENVOD KINGDOM business information required before stronger claims

**Purpose:** Collect the smallest evidence needed to approve factual service copy, directory profiles and future local SEO work. This file is a checklist, not a request to publish certificates or personal records.

## Company identity and local presence

- Confirm the approved English and Arabic legal/trading names and their relationship to the registered entity.
- Confirm the public business address, Riyadh premises, operating hours, public phone/WhatsApp and email recipient.
- Confirm which locations are actual branches, staffed facilities, partner locations or service gateways. Do not describe a port, airport or city as an ENVOD office without evidence.
- Confirm who may approve company descriptions, logos, photographs, directory listings and membership statements.

## Customs and gateway scope

For Riyadh Dry Port, Jeddah Islamic Port, King Abdulaziz Port in Dammam, airports and any land border, identify:

- Whether ENVOD itself is authorized, whether a named third-party operator performs the work, or whether the service is only document/logistics coordination.
- The current legal entity, permit or license scope where a customs brokerage or regulated role is claimed.
- Which gateways, cargo types and procedures are actually available, with any exclusions and the date last confirmed.
- The precise responsibility split for declarations, product approvals, customs examination, duty payment and cargo release.

## Exhibition logistics and ATA Carnet

- Confirm the services actually provided by ENVOD versus a venue-appointed contractor: international freight, customs coordination, venue delivery, lifting, unpacking, storage, installation, dismantling and return transport.
- Confirm the cities and venues where service can currently be arranged, and any organizer or venue approvals required.
- Supply documentary permission before stating that ENVOD served, sponsored, was appointed by or officially partnered with a named event or venue.
- Confirm the company's role in ATA Carnet work. The authorized association issues the Carnet; provide any current agreement or written authorization before claiming a more specific role.
- Confirm the current Saudi-use scope with the authorized association and authorities. Do not infer Saudi eligibility for professional equipment or commercial samples from the international ATA categories.
- Confirm who is responsible for item lists, values, serial numbers, endorsements, re-export evidence and discharge.

## Freight, transport and fleet

- Confirm current origin/destination lanes, carrier/agent relationships and whether a service is direct, partner-arranged or enquiry-only.
- Confirm vehicle counts, classes, ownership/lease/partner status, available dates, border coverage and whether GPS tracking is actually available.
- Confirm any transit-time, delivery-window, emergency, NFO or rate statements with their route, period, source and exceptions.
- For project cargo, identify actual survey, permit, escort and lifting responsibilities and the operator that performs each task.

## Warehousing and temperature-controlled services

- Confirm each facility's public location, operator, capacity, cargo restrictions and current availability.
- Provide the exact authorization evidence for bonded status and the facility/entity it covers.
- Confirm temperature and humidity ranges, monitoring method, calibration/validation records, reporting, alarm response, custody transfer and responsible operator.
- Confirm whether GDP, ISO, SFDA or another credential is held; provide holder, scope, issue/expiry dates and permission to display.
- Confirm actual WMS/ERP integrations, customer access, supported platforms and update frequency before making technology claims.

## Regulated goods and certifications

- For IATA DGR, IMDG, ADR, UN packaging, explosives or radioactive-material claims, identify current qualified people, certificates, covered classes, valid dates, permitted routes and the operator responsible.
- For SFDA, SABER, SASO, halal and other regulatory language, distinguish product/consignment requirements from company certification or authority approval.
- Confirm the exact service scope for pharmaceuticals, food, animals, dangerous goods and other regulated cargo; identify what is refused or requires specialist referral.

## Packaging and service guarantees

- Confirm whether packaging/crating is performed by ENVOD or a provider, including materials, treatment, ISPM-15 marking and destination-specific evidence.
- Confirm which guarantees, insurance arrangements, delivery commitments or service-level agreements exist in writing. Do not publish an outcome guarantee based only on an estimate or historical average.
