# 90-day SEO roadmap

No ranking, traffic, lead or Core Web Vitals outcome is guaranteed.

## Organic-growth extension

The current unified inventory has 149 opportunities, including 40 explicitly unmeasured backlog candidates; use `ksa-organic-keyword-database.csv` and separate EN/AR maps. The earlier 109-row source remains intact.

Run `backlink-outreach-plan.md` alongside the phases below: qualify the seven inspected public pathways and three chamber research candidates, approve facts/rights, and record only real submissions/publications. The linkable resources in `linkable-assets-plan.md` support outreach after publication and editorial review. No backlink count, traffic target or fictional office is an acceptance criterion.

## Days 1–14 — evidence, accurate conversion and baseline

1. Confirm legal business details, sender authorization, actual city coverage, operator/partner distinctions and permitted profiles.
2. Review the business-claims register, removing or substantiating inherited statistics, credentials and operational promises.
3. Run an authorized live enquiry test; confirm provider acceptance and separately verify the recipient inbox. Finish contact/quote failure and duplicate-submit testing.
4. Connect an approved Search Console property and GA4/GTM measurement plan. Record current first-party baseline without substituting the supplied SAL estimates.
5. Validate the 40-row benchmark provenance: report date, tool, country, device and definitions of volume/traffic/position. Retain the original file unchanged.

Acceptance: accurate visible claims, functional delivery or an explicit blocked status, stored baseline dates/filters, no PII in analytics.

## Days 15–30 — prioritize existing commercial pages

1. Review the 109-row initial opportunity map with exact-query demand evidence where available.
2. Optimize existing customs, forwarding, air/ocean, exhibition/ATA and GCC pages; keep one preferred URL per intent.
3. Use customer questions and Search Console terms for document-preparation, commodity, quote and timing content.
4. Professionally review corresponding Arabic copy, headings and metadata. Test equivalent-page switching and RTL.
5. Maintain brand clarity; obtain ownership confirmation before changing profiles or adding ambiguous aliases.

Acceptance: differentiated pages with useful EN/AR information, traceable sources and a tested server-backed enquiry path.

## Days 31–60 — justified coverage and performance

1. Validate 3PL, domestic fleet, storage/temperature control, last-mile and project capabilities before adding distinct pages.
2. Create city/gateway content only when actual operation details and unique customer needs support it. No speculative offices.
3. Publish permitted case studies or genuine operating insights instead of unsupported counters.
4. Profile LCP/INP/CLS and network/JS execution on representative mobile devices. Prioritize measured bottlenecks; preserve the cinematic presentation.
5. Improve image sizing, lazy loading, semantic headings and keyboard/error behavior.

Acceptance: substantiated expanded content, measured before/after performance results and no thin duplicate city pages.

## Days 61–90 — measure qualified business demand

1. Compare Search Console query/page impressions, clicks, CTR and average position over consistent periods, country/device filters and language groups.
2. Separate branded, generic commercial, Arabic, city, educational and competitor-navigational segments.
3. Review qualified-enquiry outcomes, not just contact-button clicks. Keep personal records out of public reports.
4. Address cannibalization, pages not indexed and low-quality traffic using observed evidence.
5. Refresh competitor estimates only with dated provenance; retain historical snapshots separately.

Acceptance: a documented trend report that distinguishes search visibility, event counts and manually qualified business enquiries. Report limitations and seasonality; do not claim causation merely from a before/after change.
