## Semantic keyword deduplication

Before adding a phrase as a new opportunity, compare it semantically with existing keywords mapped to the same page and related pages. Simple preposition, word-order, country-name, and repeated service-plus-city variations do not establish a new opportunity by themselves.

**Why:** exact-match checks can mislabel phrases such as “service Riyadh” and “service in Riyadh” as separate opportunities even when they express the same intent.

**How to apply:** use exact normalization first, then review likely intent clusters before assigning a new target page. Keep a location phrase only when it represents a materially distinct, supported target rather than a template variation.
