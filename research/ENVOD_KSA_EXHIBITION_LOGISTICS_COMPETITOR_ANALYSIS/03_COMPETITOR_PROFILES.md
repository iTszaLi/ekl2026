# Competitor Profiles — Owner-Supplied URLs Reviewed

**Updated:** 8–9 November 2026 after the owner supplied exact URLs for all ten named companies. Pages were read-only fetched directly; the IELA directory and ATA guidance were rechecked on 9 November. Company claims below remain self-published unless explicitly marked verified. No current Google rank, map position, traffic, backlink, capacity or general legal-entity audit was available.

## Direct KSA exhibition/event logistics competitors by published service evidence

### 1. BCC Logistics — Saudi unit
**Supplied home:** http://www.bcclogistics.com/  
**Saudi profile:** http://www.bcclogistics.com/About_KSA.aspx  
**Exhibitions page:** http://www.bcclogistics.com/Service.aspx?S=FAIRS%20AND%20EXHIBITIONS

The Saudi profile says BCC Saudi Arabia was established in 2013, handles shows/events/exhibitions, offers Saudi-wide delivery and is appointed for exhibitions in Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam. Its fairs page describes international trade-show logistics, customs/import paperwork, on-site handling, storage, equipment, documentation, pickups, transport and insurance. **IELA membership is independently verified:** IELA’s official directory lists “BCC Logistics KSA” as a Saudi Arabia Full member with Domestic, Export & Onsite business types. That listing does not verify specific event appointments or annual volume. BCC’s claim of more than 100 exhibitions annually and its appointment claims remain company-published; the page footer carries a 2019 copyright, so currentness needs verification. The exact supplied `http` site returned 200; an HTTPS request timed out in this check, so TLS availability is unresolved. Sampled HTML exposed only a generic title and no detected description, canonical, language or H1. [R61](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r61) [R71](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r71) [R60](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r60)

**Classification:** Direct Saudi exhibition-forwarding competitor based on the Saudi-specific pages, with all appointments/credentials treated as company claims.

### 2. BAFCO International
**Supplied home:** https://www.bafcointl.com/

BAFCO’s About page explicitly lists exhibition logistics among its specialties and describes Saudi branches in Jeddah, Riyadh, Dammam, Jubail and King Abdullah Port/Rabigh. Its services include ocean/air/land, project cargo, customs, terminals and warehousing. This establishes a direct exhibition-service claim and a broad Saudi footprint claim, but no dedicated exhibition landing page was found in the sampled navigation/service pages. Branches, owned assets and national coverage are not independently verified. Sampled homepage/about/service pages had titles, descriptions, self-canonicals, English language and three hreflang entries; the services page had one H1 and Organization/LocalBusiness schema. This is a limited HTML check, not a complete technical audit. [R62](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r62) [R60](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r60)

## What this competitor set means

- **Direct KSA event/exhibition service evidence:** BCC Saudi, BAFCO (service claim), Future Mobility/FMS, Four Winds and EFS; SMI and Khelogix are additional direct content comparators.
- **Adjacent KSA logistics/infrastructure:** Agility Logistics Parks, Maxon and Globus; SAL and Bahri from prior research.
- **Not a direct KSA exhibition competitor as supplied:** Falcon Logistics Services (Pakistan/UAE focus) and Aegis India (India-focused gas/liquid logistics).
- These classifications are about public service/geography evidence—not current rankings, market share or quality. BCC Logistics KSA's IELA membership category is verified in the association directory; other local coverage, licensing, event-appointment and capacity claims remain unverified beyond company pages.
