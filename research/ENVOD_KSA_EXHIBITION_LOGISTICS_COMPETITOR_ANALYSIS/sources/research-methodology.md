# Research methodology and access limitations

- Cut-off: 8 November 2026.
- Mode: research-only; no app/code edits, environment changes, Git operations, pushes or deployments.
- Production crawl: parsed current sitemap, fetched 94 listed URLs, recorded status/metadata/canonical/hreflang/H1/schema types and extracted same-domain links; checked 145 unique linked destinations.
- External evidence: official Google/ZATCA/ICC and company pages were retrieved read-only through direct HTTP where possible; source status, final URL, title, description and excerpt are recorded in `public-source-extracts-2026-11-08.csv`. Two Bahri requests timed out in the main extract batch; those claims are attributed to the separate direct-HTTP gap-fill and marked accordingly.
- Search result access: web search/fetch and screenshot callbacks returned HTTP 402; direct Google result HTML produced a JavaScript loader with no visible result list. Bing fallback attempts were not reliable. No current city-level Google or Bing ranks, local pack grid or AI Overview citation data are claimed.
- GSC: analyzed owner-supplied screenshot/CSV files only. Account authentication was not used; file ranges/filters are absent and totals do not reconcile.
- Candidate keywords: separated 100 phrases from the supplied master file (heuristic priority/estimated intent; volume and difficulty unmeasured) from 25 GSC query observations (undated). The CSV does not convert impressions to search volume or average position to live rank.
- Competitor evidence: company-published pages establish content themes only. Unresolved company names were not matched to guessed domains. No independent legal entity, capacity, market share, traffic, rank or backlink verification was performed.
- Interpretation: findings are labeled observed facts, owner-supplied evidence, company claims or hypotheses. No causal ranking diagnosis is asserted without matched time-series and location data.
