## Public evidence

The website presents ENVOD as Riyadh-based and the live homepage JSON-LD links the company entity to a Google Maps CID and a JCTRANS profile. These are publisher-provided entity references, not independently verified ownership, address accuracy, duplicate-profile status or current profile health. [R06](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r06) [R08](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r08)

Google’s own guidance says local results are principally based on relevance, distance and prominence. This means adding the names of Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam or Jubail to copy cannot overcome the distance component and does not justify claiming an office or local team. [R31](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r31)

## Audit checklist for the owner/authorized profile manager

- Confirm the exact business name, primary category, eligible secondary categories, phone, website and hours in the verified profile.
- Confirm the public address and whether customers are served there; do not use a virtual, borrowed or unsupported location.
- Check for duplicate or former profiles and name/address/phone inconsistencies; document the canonical profile URL/CID.
- Review service areas only where the business genuinely serves customers; a service-area setting is not a substitute for an eligible address or evidence of a branch.
- Export Profile Performance for a fixed date range and available query/location/device dimensions; separate calls, website clicks and directions.
- Request genuine, non-incentivized reviews through a consistent process; do not buy reviews or gate requests.
- Compare local map-grid positions at documented coordinates in each target city; one personal search is not a citywide rank.

## KSA city strategy

Riyadh is the only city represented as the company base in the current public site. Jeddah, Dammam/Eastern Province and Jubail can be treated as customer/service markets only when route coverage, local contacts, venue access and responsibilities are verified. Do not create “office” pages or LocalBusiness nodes for cities where no eligible premises exist. If useful, create city-specific event logistics guidance only when it contains genuinely distinct venue delivery requirements, lead times, route constraints and contact process—not duplicated keyword pages.
