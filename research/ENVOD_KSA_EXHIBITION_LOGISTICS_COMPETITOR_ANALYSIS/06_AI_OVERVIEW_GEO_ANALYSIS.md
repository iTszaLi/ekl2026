A robots allow rule permits crawling; it does not prove a crawler visited, indexed or cited the content. [R04](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r04)

- The EN/AR exhibition pages are indexable, self-canonical and language-linked in the current crawl. [R06](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r06) [R07](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r07)

Useful, original, people-first content and crawl/index eligibility remain central; appearance is not guaranteed. [R22](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r22) [R23](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r23)

## GEO content recommendations

1. **Answer the operational question directly:** what an exhibitor should prepare, which facts are needed to quote, and what remains subject to route, cargo, customs and venue confirmation.
2. **Explain ATA accurately:** ENVOD can coordinate enquiries and documentation; it is not the Carnet issuing authority. Refer users to current ZATCA/ICC sources and state that eligibility depends on the goods, route and purpose. [R32](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r32) [R33](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r33) [R34](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r34)
3. **Separate the shipment phases:** international freight, Saudi customs/temporary admission, venue delivery windows/access, on-site scope, dismantling, and return or re-export. Do not imply all services are included in every quote.
4. **Publish English and Arabic together:** equivalent facts, source links, accurate RTL layout, correct language metadata and reciprocal hreflang; the current technical crawl already shows 47 routes per language and three alternate links per URL. [R01](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r01)
5. **Use structured data only to describe visible, accurate content.** Do not add invented event participation, credentials, reviews or location entities. FAQ markup does not guarantee rich results, and Google’s FAQ rich-result appearance is restricted. [R28](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r28) [R29](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r29)
6. **Build authority through original evidence:** explain the process using official Saudi customs sources, identify what is confirmed per shipment, and publish only verified operational examples or owner-approved case studies.

## What cannot be claimed

No AI Overview inclusion, ChatGPT/Perplexity citation, answer-engine ranking, or competitor AI visibility was independently measured. Robots.txt and llms.txt are not proof of indexing. There is no special “GEO markup” that guarantees inclusion. No ranking improvement can be promised from this research.
