## Safe opportunity priorities

| Priority | Opportunity | Evidence gate | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Correct, consistent business citations | Confirm exact entity name, public phone, eligible address and profile ownership | Reconcile the verified Google Business Profile, company-owned profiles and genuinely eligible directories; correct inaccurate records rather than creating duplicates |
| P1 | Genuine logistics-network/member profiles | Confirm current membership/participation and permitted public profile | Link to the exact verified company profile and use only approved logos/claims; do not infer a membership from an affiliation logo |
| P1 | Useful event/venue resources | Confirm real shipment or vendor relationship and content permission | Contribute factual exhibitor-preparation resources to legitimate event, venue or organizer resources only when invited/eligible; clearly label sponsorship or commercial placement |
| P2 | Saudi logistics trade publications | Confirm editorial standards and a genuinely useful contribution | Pitch a sourced guide on Saudi exhibition cargo preparation, temporary admission and return shipment; avoid keyword-stuffed guest posts |
| P2 | Customer/supplier case studies | Obtain written permission and factual records | Publish a named or anonymized case study only with approved evidence, period, scope and outcomes; do not invent event names, clients or metrics |
| P3 | Chamber and business directories | Confirm actual membership, eligibility, cost and public listing/link path | Correct an existing eligible record; a paid directory listing is not an earned editorial endorsement or ranking guarantee |

## Authority content for the exhibition cluster

The highest-quality link asset is likely an accurate bilingual resource that exhibitors, event organizers and forwarders can cite: a Saudi event-cargo checklist with official ZATCA/ICC references, clear role boundaries, venue-planning inputs and a return/re-export closeout list. This is a recommendation, not evidence that a publisher will link to it.

## Prohibited or high-risk approaches

- Do not buy ranking promises, bulk directory links or private-network links.
- Do not create fake branches, city offices, event-partner logos, memberships, customer names or organizer endorsements.
- Do not use event pages as if ENVOD were an official organizer or appointed forwarder without permission.
- Do not use an event sponsorship link without proper commercial disclosure and accurate relationship wording.
- Do not treat a link from a directory as proof of operational capability or a public listing as endorsement.

## Minimum data to quantify authority

Export the Search Console Links report (top linking sites, top linked pages and top linking text); capture a date-stamped third-party link report only if the owner has an approved provider; identify root domain and canonical host; then manually review relevance, editorial control, destination status, spam risk and actual relationship. No such measurement was performed in this research-only task.
