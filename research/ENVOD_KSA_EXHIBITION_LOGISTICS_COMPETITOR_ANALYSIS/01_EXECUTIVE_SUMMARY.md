# Executive Summary

**Date:** 8–9 November 2026 | **Mode:** Read-only research

## Finding in one line

The evidence does not establish a current Google ranking loss or its cause. The live website is crawlable and bilingual, but indexation, city-level rank, AI Overview citations, Google Maps positions and backlinks remain unverified.

## Evidence-backed findings

- **Current production crawl:** 94/94 sitemap URLs return HTTP 200; 47 EN / 47 AR. All records include title, description, one H1, self-canonical, index/follow and three hreflang alternates. 145 unique same-domain linked destinations were checked and returned 200. This is a point-in-time crawl, not a Google index report. [R01](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r01) [R02](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r02)
- **Exhibition page:** The existing English page title, copy and metadata address exhibition freight, customs-document support, ATA Carnet enquiries, venue delivery and return/re-export planning. It uses qualified wording and states that routes, responsibilities, venue access and scope are confirmed per shipment. The Arabic page is live and translated. Current metadata includes the phrase “leading exhibition logistics providers in KSA”; it is not a claim that ENVOD is ranked number one. [R06](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r06) [R07](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r07)
- **Search Console:** The supplied Pages export reports the English exhibition URL at 16 clicks / 494 impressions / average position 11.33. The query export reports “exhibition shipments to saudi arabia” at 41 impressions / 0 clicks / position 11.20 and “exhibition cargo handling” at 13 / 0 / 70.46. These exports are undated and totals conflict. Average position is not a current live rank. [R12](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r12) [R13](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r13)
- **Indexing:** Owner-supplied 23 Oct snapshot lists 32 indexed; 31 crawled-not-indexed; 19 discovered-not-indexed; 3 redirects; 7 alternate pages with proper canonical. Redirect/alternate categories can be expected. The remaining 50 exclusions require URL Inspection, not bulk deletion. [R15](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r15) [R18](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r18)
- **Competitor pages and claims:** The owner-supplied URLs identify BCC Saudi, Future Mobility/FMS, Four Winds and EFS as publishing event/exhibition services; BAFCO also lists exhibition logistics. The official IELA directory verifies BCC Logistics KSA as a Saudi Full member, but no event appointment or city-rank claim was independently confirmed. Other service/location/license claims remain company-published. Validate the duplicate and evidence the business relationship; no rich-result validator was run. [R08](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r08)

## Main limitations

Google SERP/AI Overview search calls returned HTTP 402; direct Google HTML for four city-modified queries returned a JavaScript shell without visible results. The official IELA directory verified BCC Logistics KSA's membership category; other event appointment, local office and license claims remain unverified outside company pages. No authenticated Google Search Console, Business Profile, Bing Webmaster or backlink account was queried. Search volume, difficulty, city rank, competitor traffic, AI citations and link counts are not measured. The 125-row keyword database distinguishes 100 owner-supplied candidates from 25 user-exported query observations.

## Recommendation

Standardize the Search Console exports and URL Inspection evidence first. Then preserve the existing bilingual page and strengthen it with verified educational resources on temporary import/ATA Carnet process, shipment checklists, venue delivery planning and return shipments. Do not create thin city pages, claim unverified office locations, or promise ranking/AI inclusion. This report makes no website changes.
