## Executive conclusion

This review does **not** establish a measured Google-ranking loss for “best exhibition logistics in Riyadh,” nor a verified cause. The supplied Google result description is a single owner-reported snapshot, not a reproducible SERP capture; current Google search-result and AI Overview pages could not be independently observed from this environment. Search Console exports are useful but their date/filter metadata is absent and the exports do not reconcile. The current production crawl is technically healthy at the page level, but that does not prove Google has indexed or ranked those URLs.

The most defensible finding is that ENVOD already has a relevant bilingual exhibition-logistics page and a clean current URL structure. On 8 November, all **94 sitemap URLs** (47 English, 47 Arabic) returned 200; all had one H1, title, description, self-canonical, index/follow directive and three language-alternate entries. All 145 unique internal-link destinations checked also returned 200. This is strong evidence of current crawlability, not of indexation. ## Why ENVOD may not appear in the described search

The attached brief describes a Google AI Overview and results for “best exhibition logistics in Riyadh” that mix exhibition venues, organizers, directories, event entities and logistics technology. If accurate, that one snapshot is consistent with a broad or ambiguous interpretation of “exhibition logistics,” not exclusively freight-provider comparison. It does not prove that Google rejected provider intent or that the named entities outrank ENVOD in controlled searches. No result ordering, cited URLs, full AI Overview capture, location settings, device or personalization state was supplied. [R18](13_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#r18)

Other plausible explanations—local distance, query mix, indexing selection, authority, or stronger event-specific content—remain hypotheses. Current robots, sitemap, canonical and hreflang evidence does not show a current broad technical block. The historical report of Arabic 404s is followed by a 94-route bilingual crawl in November; the earlier defect should be treated as a recovered issue, not as a current finding. ## Decision

**There is not enough evidence to say ENVOD lost its Riyadh ranking, that competitors displaced it, or that a specific technical issue caused a decline.** The current page-level crawl is healthy; the supplied data show query opportunities and Google indexing exclusions that deserve controlled follow-up. AEO/GEO should be improved through useful, accurate, crawlable bilingual content and genuine expertise—not special AI markup or promises of AI Overview inclusion.
