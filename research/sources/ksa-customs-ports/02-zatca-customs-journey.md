## Relevant page text

ZATCA's page describes FASAH registration for a new importer/exporter and lists a Commercial Registration Number and ID number as requirements for that service.

The Customs Broker Authorization service allows importers/exporters, traders or non-traders, to delegate a customs broker at a specific customs port to handle customs procedures. Authorization can be limited to import, export, or cover both. For traders it can be single-use or multiple-use; for non-traders it is single-use only. Listed requirements are an active FASAH account, the customs broker's license number, and authorization duration.

The same page describes a customs declaration inquiry service, which can show declaration details and associated fees and can be searched using declaration or bill-of-lading information with the customs port specified.

## Scope and caveat

This establishes an authorization relationship and the broker's delegated procedural role; it does not say that the importer is relieved of its own documentation or compliance obligations. Read together with ZATCA's Import Instructions, the importer remains responsible for presenting goods-specific required documentation.
