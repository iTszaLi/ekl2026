# Evidence: ICC on Saudi ATA Carnet launch, roles, and Saudi scope

- **Source:** International Chamber of Commerce (ICC)
- **Title:** “Saudi Arabia and the Philippines to launch ATA operations in 2024”
- **URL:** https://iccwbo.org/news-publications/news/saudi-arabia-and-the-philippines-to-launch-ata-operations-in-2024/
- **Published:** 22 April 2024 (page metadata; modified 22 April 2024)
- **Fetched:** 17 October 2026
- **Source tier:** Tier 1 — international chamber / administrator of the ATA Carnet system

## Extracted evidence

ICC states that the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia would join its ATA Carnet International Guaranteeing Chain. The article says:

> “The implementation of ATA Carnets will be effective on 1 June 2024 for the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia …”

> “The Federation of Saudi Chambers and the Philippines Chamber of Commerce and Industry, as national guaranteeing and issuing associations were approved during a meeting of the ICC World Chambers Federation (WCF) General Council on 21 November 2023.”

It states that, from 1 June 2024, Saudi Customs would accept ATA Carnets for:

> “goods for display or use at exhibitions, fairs, meetings or similar events (Annex B1 of the Istanbul Convention).”

ICC describes the carnet generally as an international customs document for duty- and tax-free temporary admission for up to one year. The Saudi-specific description on this page is narrower: Annex B1 event goods. The general description should not be taken to mean that every category of equipment or every temporary-import purpose is covered in Saudi Arabia.
