# Evidence: Saudi Press Agency on ZATCA–Federation ATA agreement

- **Source:** Saudi Press Agency (SPA / واس)
- **Title:** “Economic / ZATCA and Federation of Saudi Chambers sign an agreement to activate temporary admission of goods”
- **URL:** https://www.spa.gov.sa/w1851565
- **Published:** 8 February 2023 (SPA page metadata and article text)
- **Fetched:** 17 October 2026
- **Source tier:** Tier 1 — official Saudi national news agency reporting a government-agency agreement

## Extracted original-language evidence

SPA reports that the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA) and the Federation of Saudi Chambers signed an agreement to activate ATA Carnet use for goods temporarily brought in and subsequently re-exported, including goods for exhibitions, seasonal markets, meetings, and similar events. The article says:

> “وبموجب الاتفاقية سيتولى اتحاد الغرف السعودية عملية إصدار دفاتر الإدخال المؤقت للسلع والبضائع والتوقيع عليها ويكون الجهة الوطنية الضامنة أمام هيئة الزكاة والضريبة والجمارك.”

**Translation:** Under the agreement, the Federation of Saudi Chambers would issue and sign temporary-admission carnets for goods and serve as the national guaranteeing body before ZATCA.

The article describes the ATA Carnet as an international customs document for temporary admission and re-export, with a stated maximum period of one year. This is a general description in the 2023 announcement, not a promise that every item, use, or shipment qualifies under Saudi rules.
