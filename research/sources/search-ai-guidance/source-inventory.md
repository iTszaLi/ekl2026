## Source-tier note

“Tier 1” here means first-party or official authority for that publisher's own technical instructions, product controls, or legal/business process. It does not mean that a vendor's recommendations independently prove search-ranking impact. Schema.org is authoritative for its vocabulary, not a search engine's ranking decisions. Saudi government portal URLs are primary sources for official business processes, not evidence of how an answer engine ranks a business.
