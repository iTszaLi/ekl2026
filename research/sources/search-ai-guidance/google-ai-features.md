## Relevant official guidance

Google says there are no additional technical requirements for AI Overviews or AI Mode beyond the requirements for appearing in Google Search, and eligibility still requires the page to be indexed and eligible to show a snippet.

> “Just because a page meets all requirements, best practices, and complies with the policies, doesn't mean that Google will crawl, index, or serve its content. Indexing and serving isn't guaranteed.”

Google's listed SEO fundamentals include allowing crawling in `robots.txt` and hosting/CDN infrastructure, internal links, useful page experience, important information in text, and keeping structured data consistent with visible page text. It also advises keeping Merchant Center and Business Profile details up to date.

> “You don't need to create new machine readable files, AI text files, or markup to appear in these features. There's also no special schema.org structured data that you need to add.”

## Interpretation / limits

Google's statement is explicitly about Google AI Overviews and AI Mode. It is not a promise about other answer engines. The no-guarantee statement is a direct reason not to promise indexing, appearance, or citations. Google's page does not endorse `llms.txt` as an appearance requirement or ranking/citation factor.
