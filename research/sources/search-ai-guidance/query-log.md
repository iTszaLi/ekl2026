# Search log: bilingual SEO, indexing, AI visibility, crawler policies, and Saudi trust

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Method:** Four distinct free public Bing Search queries were issued directly to `https://www.bing.com/search`; no paid `webSearch` callback was used. Google Search and DuckDuckGo HTML endpoints returned anti-automation challenge pages during a preliminary check. Bing returned result pages, but the first results were noisy/off-topic for several queries, so they were not treated as reliable evidence. Primary-source URLs were opened directly instead.
- **Use:** Search results were discovery only. Claims in the evidence memo rely on the official documents captured separately below, not on search snippets.
