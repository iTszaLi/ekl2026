## Interpretation

- Bing’s sampled results were materially noisy for these Arabic/English intents. They cannot support claims about Google position, rank, impressions, search demand, traffic, or links.
- The direct company pages saved alongside this file provide the substantive competitor evidence. The SERP snippets are retained to document the discovery attempt and its limitations, not to claim market visibility.
- A Bing result for “GAC - Shipping, Logistics & Marine Services” / “GAC - Shipping, logistics and marine services in India” appeared on a GAC-qualified query. This establishes only a search lead to the GAC business; the sampled result did not establish Saudi/GCC service coverage, so GAC is not counted here as a verified direct KSA competitor.
