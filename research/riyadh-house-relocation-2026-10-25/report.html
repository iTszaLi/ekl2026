Owner confirmation required before publishing stronger service promises

  1. Does ENVOD provide household moves directly, coordinate independent providers, or use both models? Which entity contracts with the customer and handles damage, complaints and claims?
  2. Which Riyadh areas and Saudi intercity routes are currently accepted? Are apartments, villas, offices and employee moves all in scope?
  3. Are packing/unpacking, furniture dismantling/reassembly, fragile-item handling, storage and oversized-lift services available? Identify provider, terms and exclusions for each.
  4. Which international routes are supported? Who prepares, reviews and submits customs documents; who confirms admissibility, duties, exemptions and destination requirements?
  5. What insurance or liability terms apply, including limits, exclusions and claims procedure? What timing, quote validity and availability can be stated accurately?
  6. What information should an enquiry form collect and retain? Keep exact addresses optional until operationally necessary; confirm access, deletion and staff-use rules before adding more personal data.
  7. Confirm the current telephone, WhatsApp and email routes remain staffed and suitable for both language versions.
