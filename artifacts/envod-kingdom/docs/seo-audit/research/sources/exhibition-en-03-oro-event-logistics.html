Every event is a supply chain wearing a tuxedo. The LED wall on a Jebel Ali shelf is worthless until it’s through the venue dock at 22:00 Tuesday — and a Riyadh show adds a border, a customs officer and a public holiday you forgot to check. **Logistics is where regional events are actually won.** We run event logistics across the UAE and Saudi Arabia as one network: production freight and customs, cross-border trucking with realistic clearance windows, warehousing and asset management between shows, guest fleets and crew transport, and reverse logistics that leaves venues the way lawyers like them. Time-critical items can fly; scenery shouldn’t have to. What paperwork does cross-border show equipment need?+ Commercial invoices with accurate HS codes and values, packing lists that match reality, and — for equipment returning home — temporary admission handled properly so you don’t pay duty on your own LED wall twice. We prepare the pack; you sign it once. Can you store our stands and assets between events?+ Yes — racked, labeled and barcode-scanned warehousing with condition photos on intake. Your 36 sqm stand from March gets redeployed in November instead of rebuilt, which is where real budget is saved. How do venue delivery slots actually work?+ Major venues run booked dock windows; miss yours and you wait behind everyone who didn’t. We book, sequence trucks to the build order, and hold a marshalling point so the dock sees one vehicle at a time — yours. Can you move 300 guests between hotels and venues?+ Yes — manifested coach and sedan fleets, boarding marshals, staggered departures matched to the run sheet, and a control sheet that knows where vehicle 7 is. Group movement is an event within the event; we produce it as one. What about night builds and road permits?+ City load restrictions and after-hours movements need municipality permits and sometimes police coordination — filed as part of the plan, not discovered at a checkpoint at 01:00. How do you keep equipment from going missing?+ Barcode discipline: scanned out, scanned in, exceptions flagged the same night with a named human attached. Shrinkage thrives on ambiguity; we remove the ambiguity. ## Put the whole move on one schedule. Send the venues, the dates and what has to travel.
