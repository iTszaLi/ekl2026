## What is an ATA Carnet?

An ATA Carnet is an international customs document used for eligible temporary movements of goods under the ATA system. Country acceptance, permitted purpose, validity and guarantee terms depend on the issuing terms and authorities. For Saudi Arabia, confirm the current accepted use and shipment eligibility before relying on a Carnet.

Where the authorities accept a Carnet for the specific goods and use, its guarantee mechanism may apply under the relevant terms. Issuing-association fees or security requirements can still apply. Customs controls, prior approvals, examination and other cargo-specific permits remain separate requirements.

- Temporary admission only where the goods and intended use are eligible
- Country acceptance and permitted use vary; confirm before dispatch
- A recognized guarantee mechanism, subject to shipment acceptance and issuing terms
- Covers multiple trips and multiple countries on a single Carnet

## What can you move on an ATA Carnet?

International ATA guidance describes broad categories such as exhibition goods, professional equipment and commercial samples. That global description does not establish Saudi eligibility for every category: confirm the destination, intended use and specific items with the authorized issuing association and customs authorities. Consumables, giveaways or goods to be sold or left behind may require another import procedure.

- Exhibition & event goods — stands, displays, samples and demo units
- Professional equipment — broadcast, film, survey, medical and test gear
- Commercial samples — products shown to buyers before an order is placed
- Tools, instruments and equipment for on-site projects and maintenance

- Share the country of issue, itinerary, event dates and planned return date.
