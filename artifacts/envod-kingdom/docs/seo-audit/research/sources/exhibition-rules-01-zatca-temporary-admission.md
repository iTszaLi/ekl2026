### Temporary Admission

Publish Date:

Source: مركز معلومات الجمارك

Page Content

- A) Conditions and Documents of Temporary Admission:


  - 1\. A letter from the importer showing the type of the consignment required for temporary admission and its duration.
  - 2\. A letter from the agency organizing the fair in certain cases.
  - 3\. The importer shall submit a detailed statement on the imported consignment (quantity, item, number, pictures of certain consignments such as gold and jewelry).
  - 4\. The port director's approval on the temporary admission.

- B) Temporary Admission Procedures:


  - 1\. The customs broker enters the temporary admission consignment information and transmits the import declaration electronically to the customs group supervisor.
  - 2\. The customs group supervisor reviews the import declaration information to verify that no remarks exist and then prints it electronically.
  - 3\. The customs group supervisor refers the import declaration to the customs inspector.
  - 4\. The customs inspector reviews the documents, examines the consignment, matches the imported goods against what has been declared by the owner, and verifies that all documents required for temporary admission are available.
  - 5\. The customs group supervisor approves the import declaration, releases the consignment, and transmits the transaction to the bank for payment of the customs duties and tying up under the deposit security.
  - 6\. All documents of the transactions will be referred to the deposit security section where they will be kept until the consignment is re-exported.
