## Global Health Exhibition Announces New Dates for 2027

The Global Health Exhibition will return on 25–28 October 2027, bringing together global healthcare leaders, investors, innovators, and policymakers under the theme “Invest in Health.”

As part of our commitment to delivering an exceptional experience that reflects the ambitions of the health sector and its partners, the upcoming edition has been rescheduled to October 2027, allowing additional time to enhance international participation, partnerships, and investment opportunities.

In 2026, the Global Health Exhibition Digital Forum will connect the global healthcare community through expert discussions, educational sessions and facilitated connections and collaboration.

We thank our partners, exhibitors, and the wider healthcare community for their continued support and look forward to welcoming them in October 2027.
