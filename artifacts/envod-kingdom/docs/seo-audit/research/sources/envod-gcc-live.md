OVERVIEW

## What is GCC Transportation?

GCC road freight connects Saudi Arabia with the other Gulf Cooperation Council states: the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman. Requests may involve full or part loads and different vehicle types. ENVOD can review the proposed origin, destination, cargo, dates and border-document requirements; route availability, carrier/operator, vehicle and customs scope must be confirmed for each shipment.

Delivery timing depends on the route, carrier schedule, border procedures, documentation and any inspections. Ask for a shipment-specific review rather than relying on a general transit estimate.

## Plan road freight before requesting a truck

Full-truckload and part-load enquiries require different planning. Cross-border freight adds border, transit and destination documentation requirements.

- Share collection and delivery cities, border route if known and required delivery window.
- Provide pallet count, dimensions, gross weight, commodity and loading equipment needs.
- Confirm whether a dry, refrigerated or specialist vehicle is required; availability and operator arrangements are confirmed per quotation.

Do not assume daily departures, owned vehicles or a guaranteed border transit time. Permits, carrier availability, cargo acceptance and border processing affect the plan.

FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

Which GCC routes can you arrange?

Tell us the origin, destination, cargo and requested dates. Route and operator availability, border documents, customs responsibilities and service scope are checked for each request; coverage at every crossing is not assumed.

Can you help review cross-border customs documents?

We can discuss the documents and responsibilities relevant to the proposed route. Requirements vary by goods and jurisdiction, and declarations, permits or release decisions remain with the responsible operators and authorities.

How long does delivery across the GCC take?

Transit time depends on the origin and destination, carrier schedule, border procedures, documents and inspections. Share the route and ready date to request a current estimate; no general delivery window is promised.
