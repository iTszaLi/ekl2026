## **Pre-Event Planning & Consultation**

* * *

- We work closely with exhibitors and event organizers to ensure all logistics are aligned with venue requirements, timelines, and regulations.
- Guidance on documentation and customs requirements
- Route and timing planning based on build-up and dismantling periods
- Risk management and cargo insurance coordination

- Advice on temporary import or ATA Carnet procedures

## **International Freight Forwarding (Air, Sea, Land)**

* * *

- Whether shipping from Europe, Asia, the GCC, or anywhere globally — we ensure safe, on-time delivery of your exhibition goods.
- Air freight for urgent items or last-minute replacements
- Sea freight (FCL/LCL) for booths, equipment, samples
- Road freight for GCC regional movements

- Special handling for fragile or high-value exhibition pieces


## **Temporary Importation & Customs Clearance**

* * *

- SMI specializes in temporary import solutions to avoid unnecessary duties and taxes for items returning to origin post-event.
- ATA Carnet processing
- Temporary customs bonds
- Support with regulatory approvals and special licenses (especially in KSA)
- Clearance coordination at all Saudi ports and venues


## **On-Site Handling & Delivery to Booth**

* * *

- We go beyond port delivery. Our team manages last-mile logistics all the way to your exhibition stand.
- Delivery to venue docks and direct booth placement
- Offloading, unpacking, and assistance with setup
- Handling of empties and storage during the event
- Repacking and return shipping post-event

## **Return & Post-Event Logistics**

* * *

- After the show, we make sure your items return safely and quickly to their original destination or next show location.
- Re-export documentation
- Coordination with origin agents for reverse logistics

- Delivery to another exhibition or warehouse if needed


## **Support for Organizers & Contractors**

* * *

- SMI also supports event organizers, freight forwarders, and stand contractors by offering:
- Dedicated coordination teams
- Consolidated cargo handling
- Equipment rental and handling (forklifts, cranes, trolleys)
- On-ground operations team throughout the event duration

- Branded service desks and warehouse support near venue (as required)
