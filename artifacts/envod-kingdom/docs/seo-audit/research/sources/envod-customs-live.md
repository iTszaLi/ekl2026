OVERVIEW

## What is Customs Clearance?

Customs clearance involves the declarations and supporting documents required for goods to enter or leave a country, together with applicable duties and taxes. Saudi gateway enquiries may involve Riyadh Dry Port, Jeddah Islamic Port or King Abdulaziz Port in Dammam. The gateway, responsible customs operator and ENVOD's available coordination scope must be confirmed for each shipment. Classification, product requirements, inspections and release decisions depend on the cargo and responsible authorities.

Product conformity, regulated-goods approvals and temporary admission are separate requirements. SABER procedures, SFDA-related requirements or ATA Carnet eligibility may apply depending on the commodity and intended use. Assistance with documentation does not replace authority approval. Clearance timing depends on complete documents, inspections and applicable government processes.

## Prepare your Saudi customs clearance enquiry

Clearance depends on the goods, transport mode and applicable approvals. Document preparation is different from approval or release by the authorities.

- Provide the commercial invoice, transport document and origin information. ZATCA lists a certificate of origin where origin is not clearly established.
- Share the commodity description, proposed HS code, importer details, gateway and expected arrival date.
- Check product-specific requirements before shipping. SABER conformity or SFDA procedures depend on the goods; they are not blanket company certifications.
- For Riyadh, Jeddah or Dammam enquiries, identify the actual airport, port or inland destination so clearance and onward transport can be planned together.

ZATCA's current import instructions address advance Fasah declarations and commodity-specific conditions. Confirm the rules for your shipment before dispatch. Release times depend on documentation, inspections and authority decisions; no fixed clearance time is promised.

### Riyadh: inland delivery and dry-port enquiries

Riyadh is inland, not a seaport. Confirm whether the shipment is arriving by air, moving directly from a seaport by road, or using an eligible dry-port/rail arrangement. Identify the actual clearance point and final delivery address before requesting onward transport.

### Jeddah: Jeddah Islamic Port enquiries

For a Jeddah Islamic Port enquiry, share the vessel/arrival information, transport documents, container or cargo details, release status and required collection window. Confirm port collection, any handling charges, delivery destination and empty-container return responsibilities rather than assuming they are included.

### Dammam: King Abdulaziz Port and the Eastern Province

For King Abdulaziz Port in Dammam, identify the terminal, cargo dimensions, release requirements and final Eastern Province or inland destination. If a rail/dry-port movement is proposed, confirm eligibility, operator arrangements and the actual clearance location before selecting the last transport leg.

These are gateway planning considerations, not a claim of ENVOD branches, terminal operations or guaranteed access. Confirm current requirements and shipment eligibility.

FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

How long does customs clearance take in Saudi Arabia?

Timing depends on shipment type, complete documentation, inspections, product approvals and authority processing. No fixed release time can be promised before those factors are reviewed.

What documents are required for import clearance?

ZATCA lists the commercial invoice and bill of lading, with a certificate of origin where origin is not clearly established. Additional documents and approvals depend on the goods; provide a packing list and commodity details for a shipment-specific review.

Do you offer ATA Carnet services?

You can enquire about ATA Carnet and temporary-import logistics coordination. Eligibility, issuance and acceptance must be confirmed with the relevant issuing association and customs authorities; not all goods or uses qualify.
