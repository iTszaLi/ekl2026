## 15 years of building connections, opportunities and knowledge

## In Saudi Arabia's construction industry

Launched in 2010 with a mission to fully equip Saudi Arabia’s construction sector amidst its huge development plans, Big 5 Construct Saudi is the Kingdom’s leading construction event.

Now taking place in Riyadh, Big 5 Construct Saudi continues to serve as a true testament to the country’s growing attractiveness in the global construction arena, bringing together industry players in support of the Kingdom’s ambitious Vision 2030 objectives.

Developed for the industry by listening to the industry, Big 5 Construct Saudi brings Big 5 brand’s 40+ years of experience and success to the Kingdom’s bustling construction and infrastructure projects market.
