Australia's Former Housing Minister on Policy and Growth

The Hon. Michael Sukkar draws on Australia's Home Guarantee Scheme to examine what works in housing policy - and what doesn't. A practical lens on affordability, supply, planning reform, and the economic consequences of getting housing right.
