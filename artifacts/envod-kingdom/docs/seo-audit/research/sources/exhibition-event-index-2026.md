## INDEX SAUDI ARABIA

**Date: 15 – 17 September 2026**

**Venue: Riyadh International Convention & Exhibition Center (RICEC)** **Timings: 4 PM - 10 PM Daily**

**URL: [https://www.index-saudi.com(opens in new tab)](https://www.index-saudi.com/)**

INDEX Saudi Arabia is the Kingdom's leading interior design and fit-out trade event. It attracts a diverse array of international and local brands that bring a rich mosaic of new ideas and trends, collectively shaping the ever-evolving landscape of interior design in the country.

Discover stunning exhibits that will inspire your next project, connect with leading designers who can bring your vision to life, and explore the latest trends and technologies that will transform your spaces.
