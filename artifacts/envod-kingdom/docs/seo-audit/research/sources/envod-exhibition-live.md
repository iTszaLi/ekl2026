## What is exhibition logistics?

Exhibition logistics is the specialised transport, customs and on-site handling of everything an exhibitor needs to present at a trade show or event — stand structures, display materials, product samples, audiovisual and technical equipment, marketing collateral and giveaways. Unlike ordinary freight, it runs to an immovable deadline: the cargo must arrive, clear customs and reach the correct hall and stand number before build-up ends.

Some exhibition goods may qualify for temporary admission under an ATA Carnet or another procedure. Saudi acceptance depends on the goods, intended use and current authority requirements; confirm eligibility, endorsements, deadlines and any separate approvals before dispatch. A Carnet does not guarantee release or remove customs controls.

- Review air, sea or road freight options for the event route, subject to carrier acceptance

- Venue-delivery planning against organizer access rules and appointed contractors
- On-site unloading, installation or dismantling only where the venue permits and a provider is confirmed
- Return-routing and re-export planning after the event

## Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ATA Carnet?

An ATA Carnet is a customs document for eligible temporary movements under the ATA system. Saudi acceptance depends on the current permitted use and the shipment; official guidance identifies eligible goods for display or use at exhibitions, fairs, meetings and similar events. The issuing association and customs authorities determine eligibility and acceptance. ENVOD can coordinate shipment information and documents but does not issue or guarantee a Carnet.

How far in advance should I plan exhibition logistics?

Start when the organizer's schedule and shipment details are available. The required lead time depends on origin, routing, cargo, approvals, carrier capacity and venue deadlines; there is no universal booking window.

Which Saudi exhibition venues can I enquire about?

Enquiries may include venues such as RICEC, Riyadh Front, Jeddah Superdome, Jeddah Center for Forums & Events and Dhahran Expo. A venue listing is planning context only; access, appointed contractors, delivery windows and service availability must be confirmed for the event.

Can you coordinate exhibition freight to Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam?

Enquiries can include these cities and other Saudi destinations. Share the origin, venue, cargo list, packed dimensions and weight, event dates and preferred freight mode. The route, carrier, customs operator, local delivery and venue-access arrangements are confirmed for the shipment.

What documents are typically requested for exhibition cargo to Saudi Arabia?

A commercial invoice, packing list, transport document and itemized cargo or equipment list are useful starting points. Certificates of origin, attestations, permits and product documents depend on the goods, importer and route. For temporary admission, confirm whether an ATA Carnet or another procedure applies with the responsible operator and authorities.

Can you arrange on-site setup or dismantling?

Share the venue, stand, event dates and requested tasks. On-site work may require organizer approval or an appointed contractor; the responsible provider and scope must be confirmed before booking.

Can you arrange temporary import without an ATA Carnet?

Alternative temporary-import routes may be available when a Carnet is not used, but requirements, guarantees, and approval depend on the goods and responsible authorities. Share the origin, event purpose, and equipment list so the applicable route can be checked.

What event information should I include in an enquiry?

Include the event name, venue and city, move-in and move-out dates, cargo list, dimensions, weight, origin, temporary-import or return needs, and any on-site tasks. This helps review the requested scope; it does not imply an event appointment or past participation.

Can you plan return shipping after the event?

Return and re-export requirements can be reviewed with the event schedule, cargo list, documents and next destination. Carrier options, venue collection, customs responsibilities and availability must be confirmed for the specific shipment.

How much does exhibition logistics cost?

Cost depends on cargo volume and weight, transport mode, origin, event dates, and requested on-site support. Share your equipment list and event details to request a no-obligation quote.

Should I use air or sea freight for exhibition cargo?

Compare the cargo, origin, event deadline, dimensions, budget and carrier options. Air freight may suit tighter schedules when capacity and acceptance are available; sea freight may be considered for larger cargo when the schedule allows for port and inland handling. Transit times and delivery arrangements depend on the route and operators.
