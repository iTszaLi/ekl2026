## Be part of Jeddah’s growing    construction market

With over **$692 billion** worth of projects are underway across Jeddah, Makkah and Madinah. Saudi Arabia ranks among the **top three global construction markets by project value** (Source: MEED), creating unprecedented demand for international construction solutions, as well as local products and services.

As a result, Jeddah Construct has continued to grow in both scale and industry significance. Following its largest edition to date in 2025, the event was also recognized with a nomination for **Best Exhibition Under 10,000 SQM** at the Middle East Event Awards, reflecting its growing impact on the Kingdom's construction sector.

The 2027 edition of Jeddah Construct will bring together **200+ exhibitors from 25+ countries, 3,500+ products** and a powerful network of **13,000+ professionals** – all in one place. The race to supply this buoyant market and support building at scale is creating profitable opportunities for manufacturers, suppliers and local agents.
